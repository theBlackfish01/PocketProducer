---
name: native-arrangement
description: Construct an editable native musical arrangement from a user's direction using Pocket Producer's validated tools.
---

Text alone is sufficient; owned recordings are optional. Start with `record_native_plan`: capture form, sound goals, explicit constraints and development tasks. The plan is durable progress, not accepted music. Use `advance_native_stage` as construction moves through building, refining and reviewed. A reviewed stage needs inspection of at least two current sections after the last change. On a resumed request, inspect the current document and saved plan before acting; do not repeat confirmed steps.

`compose_native_form` can establish an original meter, section lengths, instrument roles, explicit motif notes and placements, processing and automation. It is an optional starting form, not a fixed template or a ceiling on complexity. Build further in small `apply_native_batch` steps: `harmonizeSection` writes exact chord voicings over a cycle, and `sequenceSectionPattern` writes expressive velocity and timing variation on a MIDI-capable instrument. Use individual motif and note operations for details that do not fit those transforms. Read the pinned workspace or use catalogue discovery when device details matter. Catalogue discovery is not permission to write an entity. Keep stable IDs and step keys for further edits. Inspect the actual diffs and material in at least two sections; refine omissions before ending. A tool error is feedback: inspect current state and correct a definite failure with a fresh step key; do not reissue an uncertain external effect.

Use sections to give the piece a beginning, development and ending. Prefer distinct roles and instrument colors over copies of one phrase. Define motifs as reusable note relationships, place them in sections, then vary later occurrences deliberately. Inspect a section's onset, overlap and range measurements before declaring its development complete. `developSectionNotes` can alter notes in one section while preserving crossing material elsewhere. `silenceSectionClips` and `setSectionClipGain` split once-only and exactly representable looping clips at section edges while preserving source phase elsewhere; pass `regionId` to edit one clip rather than every clip on that part. A loop whose sample duration cannot map to an integer tick is rejected instead of approximated. `moveSectionClip` retimes one fully contained clip without changing its source offset; `shiftSectionClip` shifts and crops only the chosen section of a crossing clip, retaining original phase and both outside intervals. `editSectionAutomation` splices stepped/linear points with exact boundary values. Sloped easing cannot be split without changing its behavior outside the selection. Beatbox8 is an on/off drum grid: notes use only MIDI 36/38/42/46, sixteenth-step beat positions, quarter-beat duration and velocity 1. Pitched instruments can use expressive velocity and duration. Device/effect parameter names and ranges are in `native-sound-design` and the capability inspector. Route only through supported operations; if a requested device or sample cannot be validated, choose a supported alternative and say so.

For a precise answer or changed ending in only one placement, inspect its original motif and use `varyMotifInstance.noteEdits` with source-note IDs. Omit, move, repitch, resize or reshape the specific notes in a cloned motif; other placements keep their original notes. An unchanged edit is rejected.

Owned sources are optional. A placed interval is only “placed/referenced,” never “audibly used” until its selected bytes and a future render are checked. Do not invent sample IDs, presets or rights metadata. Make assumptions explicit in the structural explanation. No native audio is available in this milestone; do not claim to have listened or judged mix quality.

Read `references/creative-development.md` when deciding density, palette, a defining event or development. Use `search_native_examples` and `read_native_example` selectively; their original canonical forms are unheard references, not templates. Save selected reference IDs and exact chosen resource identities through `record_native_creative_state` before large mutations so they survive context compaction and restart.
