---
name: native-sound-design
description: Choose mapped Nexus instrument/effect parameters and owned-source placements without claiming audio that has not been heard.
---

`setParallelChain` creates a part-level dry/wet splitter and ordered wet effects. For a genuinely shared drum path, route related parts into one mixer group, then use `setGroupParallel` with ordered compressor/tube wet effects. Group insert effects also form a serial chain between insert output and input. `addGroupEffect`, `replaceGroupEffect`, `moveGroupEffect` and `removeGroupEffect` maintain that chain; `setGroupMix` changes level/pan/parent, and group automation addresses gain, pan, compressor, `parallel.wetMix` or `effect.<id>.<field>`. A wetMix of 0.3 is a structural blend setting, not a measured 30% perceived loudness. Inspect the route and dry/wet cables before describing it.

The pinned Nexus adapter supports Heisenberg, Pulverisateur, Gakki and Beatbox8 plus delay, pitch delay, chorus, tube saturation, reverb, compressor, EQ and auto-filter. A part routes instrument → ordered effects → mixer channel. Groups share insert/parallel processing and a bounded sidechain source; parts can send independently to one reverb and one delay return, with master gain/pan/limiter. Automation targets channel gain/pan, supported device/effect settings (including effects in a part's parallel branch), aux-send gain and mapped group settings. Choose parameters for a musical reason and inspect `inspect_native_capability` for SDK ranges. Never invent field names. Useful mapped examples: `heisenberg.operatorC.modulationFactorA` 0–1 (FM phase modulation), `heisenberg.operatorC.frequencyOffsetHz` -9999.99–9999.99 Hz, `heisenberg.operatorC.detuneFactor` 0–1, `heisenberg.operatorC.waveformIndex` 1–49, `heisenberg.filter.cutoffFrequencyHz` 33–22050 Hz, `pulverisateur.audio.drive` 0–1, `stompboxTube.drive` 0.1–12, `stompboxChorus.lfoFrequencyHz` 0.1–5 Hz, `stompboxPitchDelay.tuneFactor` -1–1. These numeric scales are not interchangeable. Beatbox8 voice shaping changes tone/gain/decay, not its boolean-step velocity; choose Gakki or another MIDI-capable part for expressive ghost timing and velocity. The form tool accepts mapped `parameters` records on devices/effects.

Search `search_native_resources` for selected owned WAVs and local parameter recipes. Inspect a candidate with `inspect_owned_source`: its timed RMS/activity bins are measurements, not semantic listening. Choose an interval from actual activity rather than taking the opening by default. Supply its asset ID, selected offset/duration, timeline bars and explicit once/loop mode; the application fills its hash and rights from server-owned data. Connected Audiotool sample and instrument-preset searches are separate; `search_gm_sounds` can discover named Gakki sounds, but applying one still needs exact preset resolution and its content fingerprint. A preset's labels do not pin its sound. Native synchronization uploads ready sample bytes with a durable identity and uses the SDK's `insertSample` offset/loop mapping. The remote upload/readback flow has only offline contract evidence until an authorized live sync succeeds. Never invent an Audiotool sample ID or claim that this interval was heard in a native mix. "Preset recipe" means explicit validated local instrument/effect parameters, not an uploaded or selected Audiotool preset.
