---
name: native-sound-design
description: Choose mapped Nexus instrument/effect parameters and owned-source placements without claiming audio that has not been heard.
---

`setParallelChain` creates a real part-level full-power dry/wet splitter, ordered wet effects and a bounded blend merger before that part's mixer channel. A tube/compressor branch can add parallel drum warmth. It is not a shared parallel drum bus; group output effects are not mapped. Keep the dry and wet identities within the selected part, and inspect routing evidence before describing this processing structurally.

The pinned Nexus adapter supports Heisenberg, Pulverisateur, Gakki and Beatbox8 plus delay, pitch delay, chorus, tube saturation, reverb, compressor, EQ and auto-filter. A part routes instrument → ordered effects → mixer channel. Validated groups can share a compressor and bounded sidechain source; parts can send independently to one reverb and one delay return, with a master gain/pan/limiter. Do not call that routing true parallel saturation: a separate independently blended wet drum bus is not mapped. Automation targets channel gain/pan, supported device/effect settings or aux-send gain. Choose parameters for a musical reason, and inspect `inspect_native_capability` for SDK ranges. Never invent field names. Useful mapped examples: `heisenberg.operatorC.modulationFactorA` 0–1 (FM phase modulation), `heisenberg.operatorC.waveformIndex` 1–49, `heisenberg.filter.cutoffFrequencyHz` 33–22050, `pulverisateur.audio.drive` 0–1, `stompboxTube.drive` 0.1–12, `stompboxChorus.lfoFrequencyHz` 0.1–5, `stompboxPitchDelay.tuneFactor` -1–1. Beatbox8 voice shaping changes tone/gain/decay, not its boolean-step velocity. The form tool accepts mapped `parameters` records on devices/effects.

Search `search_native_resources` for selected owned WAVs and local parameter recipes. Inspect a candidate with `inspect_owned_source`: its timed RMS/activity bins are measurements, not semantic listening. Choose an interval from actual activity rather than taking the opening by default. Supply its asset ID, selected offset/duration, timeline bars and explicit once/loop mode; the application fills its hash and rights from server-owned data. Connected Audiotool sample and instrument-preset searches are separate; `search_gm_sounds` can discover named Gakki sounds, but applying one still needs exact preset resolution and its content fingerprint. A preset's labels do not pin its sound. Native synchronization uploads ready sample bytes with a durable identity and uses the SDK's `insertSample` offset/loop mapping. The remote upload/readback flow has only offline contract evidence until an authorized live sync succeeds. Never invent an Audiotool sample ID or claim that this interval was heard in a native mix. "Preset recipe" means explicit validated local instrument/effect parameters, not an uploaded or selected Audiotool preset.
