---
name: native-sound-design
description: Choose mapped Nexus instrument/effect parameters and owned-source placements without claiming audio that has not been heard.
---

The pinned Nexus adapter supports Heisenberg, Pulverisateur, Gakki and Beatbox8 plus delay, reverb, compressor, EQ and auto-filter. A part routes instrument → ordered effects → mixer channel; automation can target channel gain/pan or an auto-filter cutoff. Choose parameters for a musical reason, and inspect `inspect_native_capability` for SDK ranges. Never invent field names. Useful mapped examples: `heisenberg.operatorA.gain` 0–1, `heisenberg.filter.cutoffFrequencyHz` 33–22050, `pulverisateur.filter.cutoffFrequencyHz` 18–15500, `stompboxDelay.feedbackFactor` 0–1, `stompboxReverb.roomSizeFactor` 0–1. The form tool accepts these as a `parameters` record on the device or effect.

Search `search_native_resources` for selected owned WAVs and local parameter recipes. Inspect a candidate with `inspect_owned_source`: its timed RMS/activity bins are measurements, not semantic listening. Choose an interval from actual activity rather than taking the opening by default. Supply its asset ID, selected offset/duration, timeline bars and explicit once/loop mode; the application fills its hash and rights from server-owned data. Native synchronization uploads ready sample bytes with a durable identity and uses the SDK's `insertSample` offset/loop mapping. The remote upload/readback flow has only offline contract evidence until an authorized live sync succeeds. Never invent an Audiotool sample ID or claim that this interval was heard in a native mix. "Preset recipe" means explicit validated local instrument/effect parameters, not an uploaded or selected Audiotool preset.
