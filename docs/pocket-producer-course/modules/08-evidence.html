Know what the tests prove—and what they do not.

Pocket Producer protects provider keys, source data, accepted versions, and spend. Its local tests are intentionally provider-free; real-model artistry and current live remote fidelity remain separate questions.

One sentence to remember

Scripted construction proves the workflow can enforce its contract, not that a live model wrote a great song.

Live v8 mapping-range fidelity, source-bearing remote sync, a successful Gemini audio-analysis response, native heard output, broader real-model creative benchmarks, physical-phone keyboard/safe areas, and Firefox/WebKit remain unverified or deliberately deferred. The operating guardrails

Keys stay on the serverThe ignored root environment file is loaded and validated by server config. Browser bundles receive status, never OpenAI/Gemini/Gateway credentials. Development identity is loopback-only and refused in production; separate visitor sign-in is not delivered.
External effects are ledgeredA stable effect identity is reserved before dispatch. Known usage is charged once; an ambiguous post-dispatch result retains a reservation instead of becoming a free retry. Combined, provider and owner allowances all apply.
Technical ceilings are not permissionStandard/Extended profiles bound calls, input/output, time, review attempts and per-job cost. They do not authorize spending their maximum. The separate overall cap was raised through explicit prior authorization to US$13; provider caps do not add to it.
Remaining allowance is an upper boundA 27 September read-only check returned US$7.693719 spent or reserved of US$13, leaving at most US$5.306281. Four Gemini effects have unknown liability, as does one OpenAI effect; exact remaining billable balance is not known. This course update made no provider request.
