01 / The product contract

One composition workspace

At Start, describe a piece, optionally choose an owned WAV, select a producer and a bounded creation profile. Once the request is durably accepted, Producer shows the saved approach beside the growing score. Inspect, revise, compare or explicitly copy a version to Audiotool. There is no second Playable editor or generated-audio history.

Switch the product lens

Compare saved music with the optional recordings used to make it.

A native v2 score, not a recording

Sections, parts, exact notes, reusable motifs, source clips, effects, routing, and automation are saved as an immutable document. You can inspect and revise its structure, compare versions, and explicitly synchronize an editable Audiotool project. Native rendering and playback are intentionally deferred.

No native play button Symbolic inspection

Your recordings, not a preview of the piece

Upload or record an owned sound under Sounds, audition it with labeled seek and volume controls, and select it for your next direction. This plays the original source only. It does not render or play the constructed composition.

The user-facing promise

Construct, inspect, revise

A direction drives a real Deep Agent's validated musical operations. The user can inspect the saved result, preserve parts, and compare immutable alternatives. The score must never imply sound measurements it does not have.

The honesty rule

Editable does not mean audible

A valid SDK document or an Audiotool project link is evidence of structure and mapping. It is not proof the native piece rendered, played, sounded good, or used a source with independent rights.
