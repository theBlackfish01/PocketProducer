## Run envelope

The chosen **Standard** or **Extended** profile is captured with each new native job in its private request, including model, pricing evidence, reasoning effort, call/input/output limits, deadline and per-job ceiling. A restart uses that captured profile; config changes do not silently increase an old request's allowance. Standard targets 80 model calls, a 128k *conservative UTF-8/framing input bound*, 16,384 configured maximum output tokens, 30 minutes and US$5 per logical job. Extended targets 80 calls, 160k input, 32,768 configured maximum output, 60 minutes and US$15. The actual preconstruction response/reservation ceiling is 12,288 (Standard) or 20,480 (Extended); after confirmed construction it is 8,192 or 12,288. Reasoning tokens share that response ceiling. Environment limits may make all of these smaller. Configurable installation maxima are 300 calls, 256k input-bound units, 65,536 output tokens, six hours and US$100 per job, but defaults remain lower. The separate installation-wide `INITIAL_BUILD_API_BUDGET_USD` still defaults to US$5 and can only be raised explicitly; it is not reset by a new job or profile choice. Unknown provider outcomes retain their reservation; a known truncated response cannot dispatch its partial tool arguments. For the captured model price table, ordinary input, cache reads, cache writes and output have distinct prices; only provider-reported cache reads/writes receive their respective prices. Reservations conservatively treat input as a possible cache write. The room's next-call figure is a lower bound, not a quote.

These engineering envelopes are **not permission to spend** either proposed amount. New jobs default to GPT-6 Sol as of September 27; existing jobs retain their captured Astra or other configured model and price. Normal tests clear keys or use injected models and do not contact providers. A future live creative comparison needs a separate numeric allowance and no unresolved accounting liability. `pnpm budget:status` reads the ledger without spending.

## Producer and durable truth

### Finishing and bounded continuation — 2026-09-27

The producer receives a compact outstanding-requirements checklist from the same canonical/plan completion checks used by acceptance. Durable producer-dispatch counts (excluding critic calls) introduce completion guidance at 30 and final-requirement focus at 45 turns. Finishing before 50 is a target, not a completion bypass or hard stop. Coherent validated batches are encouraged; final inspection, review, protections and accounting remain mandatory. Standard's larger 80-call safety ceiling does not increase its four critic attempts, $5 job target or any installation-wide authorization. Old captured profiles remain unchanged until an explicit extension is accepted.

Only bounded successful local skill/example/recipe/capability reads are retained across compaction and restored from this job's existing checkpoints on continuation (12 entries, 24,000 serialized characters, 6,000 characters per result). Results already present in recent model history are not duplicated. Mutable score reads, provider availability, errors and authorization are not cached as current truth. The model still uses validated tools; truncated guidance can be reread.

Repeated identical musical/plan/checklist/inspection state without new successful tool evidence warns after six turns and pauses recoverably after twelve. New evidence or confirmed work resets the streak. This is a narrow loop guard, not a musical-quality judgment or a proof that almost all briefs finish before 50. Optional recalled guidance is removed oldest-first when the existing conservative input guard would be exceeded; exact brief, current state/plan and recent read/error history keep priority. If these alone exceed the guard, the request still pauses without provider dispatch. Ordinary cost, deadline, graph and call guards still bound execution and retain safe work. Explicit continuation preserves job/effect/batch identities; it neither resets accumulated spend nor copies music to Audiotool.

The production Deep Agent can record a form/sound/constraint/development plan in `native_job_plan`, advance a stage, read the current workspace, inspect individual parts/motifs/sections and owned-source measurements, search local and connected resources, compose an optional initial form and apply small validated musical batches. Compact creative state retains chosen resource IDs and optional configuration hashes, density intent, guidance references, decisions, definite failures, unfinished tasks and ID-grounded promise links. Three substantial original scenes (8-bar groove, 16-bar build/arrival, sparse cross-instrument motif handoff) replace the six old generic one-part references; they can be searched/read selectively and are neither templates nor heard evidence. The plan and confirmed operation ledger survive worker restart; a changed plan resets its review status. A fresh graph attempt is made after a safe pause while the same job, step/effect identities and accounting continue, including a known zero-musical-step budget pause. The plan is progress; only a checked immutable revision is accepted. A real-model run must review two current sections and obtain a valid, separately accounted symbolic editor review on the final document hash before recording completion. The captured profile permits up to four Standard or six Extended review attempts, still within the job/site allowance; invalid/exhausted reviews remain visibly unavailable and cannot become clean approval. Scripted models traverse the same tools but do not prove artistic quality. Context compaction retains the original brief and ordered read/error evidence, while compact confirmed state **and the durable plan** are refreshed at each model dispatch. Exact brief windows are retrievable with `read_native_brief`; long text is not copied into every workspace result.

Hard role constraints are conservatively extracted from each role mention and its directive span: “Do not change bass” and “keep melody and bass” preserve named saved parts, while “no drums or bass” excludes their construction. A localized exclusion such as “no drums in intro” applies only to that section while a later chorus request can still require percussion. A role entrance such as “bring in chords” is not itself a section name. Explicit BPM, meter, section spans/lengths, specified role entrances and requested shared parallel drums, sidechain or changing controls are checked against the actual document. An explicitly spelled chord chain such as `Dm9–Bbmaj7–Fmaj9–Cadd9` is checked as ordered bar-level harmony/bass pitch classes, including defining thirds/extensions but allowing omitted fifths. This is a falsifiable symbolic check, not proof of voice-leading quality, correct key throughout or audible harmony. A revision must change the requested role and retain explicitly preserved material; named part hashes and a uniquely evidenced melodic theme family are checked against the saved base version. Section-scoped preservation compares actual notes, clips, automation and global sound dependencies. Ambiguous durations, multiple plausible themes and subjective goals are not silently guessed. This bounded parser cannot certify taste, tension, mix perception or every phrasing variation. Real-model review and future listening are separate evidence levels.

## Current validated controls

| Area | Local operation / mapping | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Musical form | Tempo, meter, up to 128 bars / 24 sections, parts, exact MIDI notes, motifs, local variations and section edits; one motif placement can receive exact source-note omissions/onset/pitch/duration/velocity edits without changing another placement | Structural timing; no audible judgment |
| Harmony and groove | `harmonizeSection` writes model-chosen exact polyphonic voicings over a repeated cycle; `sequenceSectionPattern` writes pitch, velocity, duration and bounded timing offsets within a section | Best with MIDI-capable instruments; Beatbox8 remains boolean-step only |
| Instruments | Heisenberg A–D waveforms, frequency offset in Hz, detune/modulation links and their supported automation, selected envelopes/LFO, Pulverisateur drive/filter, Gakki with pinned sound discovery, Beatbox8 voice shaping | Only allow-listed SDK ranges mapped into real constructor/automation fields; Beatbox8 steps have no velocity expression |
| Effects | Ordered per-part compressor, EQ, filter, reverb, delay, pitch delay, chorus and tube saturation; per-part dry/wet split; automation of supported serial **and parallel** effect fields | Structural control, not measured heard quality |
| Shared routing | Groups have ordered serial inserts, an independently blended shared dry/wet branch, compressor/sidechain, level/pan/parent routing and automation. Part sends feed shared reverb/delay returns; master gain/pan/limiter remain | Eight groups, bounded chains and SDK-supported routing, not an arbitrary bus graph |
| Local revisions | Crossing notes/motif instances, once-only and exactly-timed looping clips, section-only silence/gain of all clips or one named clip, contained clip moves, crossing-clip section shifts with exact outside phase, stepped/linear automation with retained boundary values | A shifted crossing clip is cropped at section edges; sloped easing splits and noninteger loop cycles are rejected rather than approximated |
| Resources | Owned WAV source regions and resolved Audiotool sample/preset identities with fingerprint checks; seven versioned local parameter recipes with configuration hashes, use/failure guidance and explicit unheard status; bounded library metadata filtering, on-demand PCM16/24/Float32 WAV measurement, exact once-only section-local sample slice sequencing and optional byte-hash pinning | Search metadata is briefly cached only within one authenticated SDK client object; authoritative identity and hash are rechecked at construction/synchronization. The captured profile bounds optional Gemini shortlist opinions to two Standard or three Extended distinct short WAVs (older jobs default to two), still under the same paid-effect and site ceilings. Opinions are separate from facts, owner-scoped across jobs by submitted bytes/model/prompt, with a cross-worker lock and unconfirmed-effect fence. Subsecond hits are repeated unchanged four times at one-second spacing for analysis; the derivative hash is distinct from the original. Unknown effects fence continuation; no inferred license, full-mix hearing or new live permission/readback result |
